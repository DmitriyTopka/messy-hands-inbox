**Messy Hands Inbox** - a Spectacles Lens for when your hands are in dough and three people text you at once.

One card lies on the counter, just right of your work. You glance at it, pick whose message to read, say the gist of your reply, see a clean version, and send. Every voice command also has a big pinch button.

Built with plan-lens and CLAD in Lens Studio, 6 of 6 LEAF tests pass. Shown in the preview only, no Spectacles.

Video: https://youtu.be/zjj550QKkAc
Project: https://devpost.com/software/messy-hands-inbox

What I'd like feedback on:
1. Does the idea read as "inbox for busy hands" or as "another kitchen lens"?
2. The card sits on the counter, down and to the right. Would you want it closer to eye level?
3. Voice is off in the demo (preview needs a Snap login). Does pinch-only hurt the pitch?
